# Execution Proof Public Beta Overview

## Current status

Execution Proof is a provider-independent local beta that keeps task state and completion authority outside model prose.

The public model separates:

- the exact task object and required deliverables
- recorded events and evidence
- authorization from execution
- current evidence from stale evidence
- deterministic verification from semantic review
- a model's completion statement from external completion status

The project has an internally tested local-controller record. Fresh current-build validation is required. No trusted remote producer, customer deployment, blind replication, or external validation is established.

## Recorded internal comparison history

A bounded 12-fixture internal comparison recorded:

- 9 wins
- 0 losses
- 3 ties
- 0 false passes for the candidate
- 5 false passes for the comparison baseline

Additional internal records report:

- 40 of 40 candidate results across three Frozen-40 repeats
- 20 of 40 for a historical adapter, including 19 false passes
- 30 catalogued drift events in the current adverse-condition bank

These are recorded internal test results. They were not blind, were not externally replicated, and are not customer validation. Historical campaign results must not be treated as proof that every current build has passed the same campaign.

## What the evidence supports

The bounded records support a narrower statement: explicit state and evidence checks caught false-completion conditions that comparison baselines accepted within the named internal fixtures.

They do not establish performance across arbitrary models, tools, tasks, organizations, or production environments.

## Current operating boundary

The local deterministic-artifact lane can record evidence and evaluate named checks. Remote receipts, account-state readback, and semantic acceptance require separately trusted producers or owner-managed review paths.

The beta does not transfer private control grammar, registry definitions, test logic, trust-adapter logic, collector commands, or completion thresholds through this repository.

## Evidence classification

- **Evidence class:** Bounded internal technical evidence
- **Object:** Local Execution Proof controller and named internal fixtures
- **Commercial status:** No customer deployment or paid software demand established
- **Production status:** Not established
- **Independent replication:** Not established
- **External validation:** Not established
