# Sigil One

Sigil One is a live tool that helps people see likely crypto tax exposure before they sell.

It is decision-support and tax-prep infrastructure, not filing software.

The paid output is a CPA-ready handoff bundle built from the saved snapshot and assumptions.

Manual input is intentional. The goal is consequence clarity and clean assumptions, not blind automation.

## Current workflow
- estimate the exposure
- save the snapshot
- export the handoff bundle
- verify the record later

## What I’m learning
One of the biggest product mistakes is trying to build the broad portfolio tool too early.

The sharper wedge has been:

help the user understand the likely tax consequence before the sale happens

That’s where the uncertainty is highest and where the product is most useful.
