# Dylan Fratangeli | Sigil One Public Proof

Public proof surface for work by Dylan Fratangeli under Sigil Systems.

This folder exists to make the substance behind Sigil One easier to verify in public without publishing the full private control layer behind GoldenState and FSP. The goal is not to release the entire internal archive. The goal is to surface enough inspectable material to establish technical seriousness, artifact depth, and clear boundaries.

## What this proof surface covers

- **Sigil One**
  Product layer. A decision-support system focused on crypto tax clarity.

- **GoldenState**
  Runtime authority and control framing used to constrain execution quality and reduce drift.

- **FSP**
  Formal validation and control work related to state, projection, recovery, contradiction, and completion handling.

- **This folder**
  Public verification layer. A curated proof surface, not the full private archive.

## Included artifacts

### 1. `PUBLIC_PROOF_NOTE.md`
A bridge note explaining why this proof surface exists, what is public, what remains private, and how to read the folder.

### 2. `RECOVERY_VALIDATION_PUBLIC.md`
A public-safe architecture note showing the recovery validation concept, the nine-field model, the pass/fail/blocked outcome structure, and the evidence-first constraints behind it.

### 3. `Recovery_Validator_Core_v1_Package.txt`
A compact validator artifact containing the short brief, public one-page spec, outcome contract, high-level rule surface, minimal procedure, and evidence minimum.

## What is intentionally not published here

This folder does not publish the full private control grammar behind GoldenState and FSP.

That includes:
- full startup and runtime boot surfaces
- full authority ordering
- full validator dispatch surfaces
- exact transition logic
- exact completion thresholds
- exact drift and recovery control details

Those materials exist privately. They are not required for a first public proof surface.

## Why this exists

Public discussion can easily collapse into one of two bad states:
- claims without inspectable artifacts
- overexposure of internals before packaging is mature

This folder is designed to avoid both.

It gives enough evidence to show that substantive work exists while preserving the parts that still matter for controlled release, commercialization, and later integration.
