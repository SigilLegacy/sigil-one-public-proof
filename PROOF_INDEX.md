## Evidence interpretation rules

- A model statement is a claim, not completion evidence.
- A dated internal result applies only to the named test, package, host, and boundary.
- Internal technical evidence is not customer validation.
- Completed work is not paid demand.
- A live product is not proof of a verified external purchasing cohort.
- Planned architecture is not a built product.
- Missing or conflicting evidence remains unresolved.
