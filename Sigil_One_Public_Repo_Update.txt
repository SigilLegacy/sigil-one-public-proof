# Sigil One

Sigil One is a live, crypto-first, multi-asset tax-exposure planning, evidence-preservation, and CPA-handoff system.

The primary wedge is still helping someone understand likely crypto tax exposure before a sale. Crypto has the deepest source-evidence and transaction-lifecycle workflow, but the live product also supports manual holdings and shared planning workflows for Real Estate, Precious Metals, and Investments.

Sigil One is decision-support and tax-prep infrastructure. It is not tax filing software, does not provide official tax advice, and does not connect to wallets or exchanges in v1.

## Current record model

Sigil One separates records by what they are allowed to mean:

- open lots and planning records
- staged CSV source evidence
- durable transaction evidence
- saved snapshots and assumptions
- recorded manual disposals
- export and verification artifacts

A projected sale price is not actual proceeds.
A CSV row is not automatically a current holding.
A disposal record does not silently rewrite the original holding.
Unknown basis remains unknown instead of being forced to zero.

## Current intake paths

Users can:

- enter holdings manually across Crypto, Real Estate, Precious Metals, and Investments
- upload supported crypto CSV files
- stage and review imported rows before confirmation
- inspect warnings and possible duplicates
- select which eligible rows to confirm
- preserve blocked or incomplete rows for later review
- keep source provenance attached to confirmed records

CSV import is controlled source-evidence intake, not blind synchronization.

## Current workflow

Manual and imported records:
- enter or upload records
- stage and review source evidence
- separate open lots from transaction evidence
- confirm eligible records intentionally

Planning and decision support:
- set a projected sale price
- estimate likely exposure under the selected assumptions
- save the scenario as a snapshot
- review Export Readiness before purchase

Disposal and handoff:
- preserve source-backed disposal evidence
- record a manual disposal when needed
- export the report that matches the question
- verify preserved records later

## Current paid artifact classes

Sigil One currently has three separate paid artifact paths:

1. Snapshot Tax Handoff
   - Tax Handoff PDF
   - Tax Handoff CSV
   - Proof Archive and verification material

2. Holdings PDF
   - answers: What do I still own?
   - includes eligible current open lots and planning records
   - excludes transaction evidence and realized-disposal history

3. CPA Disposal Report
   - answers: What did I sell?
   - includes eligible snapshot sales, confirmed CSV disposal evidence, and recorded manual disposals
   - keeps unknown or incomplete basis visible rather than fabricating certainty

Each artifact has its own scope and purchase path.

## Asset-category boundary

The live application supports:

- Crypto
- Real Estate
- Precious Metals
- Investments

Crypto remains the deepest and primary implementation for CSV intake, transaction evidence, and lifecycle classification.

Real Estate, Precious Metals, and Investments support manual holdings and the shared generic capital-gains planning model. They do not yet apply category-specific tax doctrines such as depreciation recapture, collectibles treatment, wash-sale rules, qualified-dividend treatment, or similar specialized rules.

## Current limitations

Sigil One does not currently claim to provide:

- tax filing
- official tax advice
- wallet or exchange synchronization
- complete category-specific tax treatment
- automatic partial-lot splitting
- automatic mutation of a holding after a disposal record
- complete exchange-pairing doctrine
- complete derivative-specific doctrine
- complete income and reward treatment
- final reconciliation where source evidence or basis is missing

## Current focus

The immediate product focus remains:

- making the crypto decision-support flow clearer
- improving source-evidence review
- keeping projected and realized values separate
- making paid artifact scope obvious before purchase
- reducing user hesitation around assumptions, readiness, and CPA handoff
- correcting category and copy drift where the live product and public description disagree

The broader architecture is multi-asset, but the market wedge remains narrow:

help users understand likely crypto tax exposure before selling, preserve the evidence and assumptions behind the decision, and create a cleaner record for CPA review.
